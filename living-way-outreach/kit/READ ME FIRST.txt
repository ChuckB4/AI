==============================================================
   LIVING WAY UPC - OUTREACH HELPER
   Quick Start for Don Taylor
==============================================================

HOW TO START (this is all you have to do):

   1. Double-click the file named:   Start Here

   2. A menu will open. Type a number and press Enter.

   That's it. You never have to type any computer commands.


WHAT THE MENU DOES:

   1 = Import your CHURCH MEMBER list.
       (When you get the member spreadsheet, pick this. The program
        will ask you to drag the file in. It keeps your members
        separate from the community contacts.)

   2 = Import an OUTREACH list.
       (Any list of people met at an event. Same easy steps.)

   3 = See where things stand - a quick summary of your numbers.

   4 = Open the data folder, where the spreadsheets live.
       You can open or print them from there.

   5 = Read this guide again.

   6 = Events. Add any event (name, date, time, place). Reuse this
       for every outreach, door-knock, or church event - forever.

   7 = Campaigns. Group one or more events together (for example,
       Saturday's outreach PLUS a new upcoming event).

   8 = Facebook posts. The program WRITES a finished post for your
       event. It copies it to your clipboard. You open Facebook and
       paste it (Ctrl+V), then press Post. You can also mark posts
       as "posted" so you remember what's done.
       IMPORTANT: the program does NOT post to Facebook by itself.
       You paste it in. (That keeps the church's page safe and is
       the only way allowed by Facebook's rules.)

   9 = Team Messages. This is where you build and run your teams:
       - Assign each volunteer to a team as a CAPTAIN or a MEMBER.
       - "View my teams" shows every team with its captain + members.
       - Write a REMINDER / recruitment text and send to everyone,
         one team, or several teams (you type the team names).
       - Write a "Share our Facebook post" text with the post link,
         asking members to share it with their friends and family.
       - Print a phone list for any team to text them quickly.
       As always: it writes the message and gathers the numbers.
       YOU send it from your phone. It never texts anyone by itself.

   10 = Field Map & Team Chat. All FREE, works on every phone.
       - Step-by-step setup for a free SHARED MAP (Google My Maps)
         where your team drops pins: "served here", "good door-knock",
         "come back later". Everyone sees the same map on their phones.
       - Step-by-step setup for a free TEAM CHAT (GroupMe/WhatsApp/
         group text) so you get instant notifications in the field.
       - Record those pins here too, so you keep a permanent history.
       NOTE: this marks PLACES only. It does NOT track or follow any
       person's location. That keeps everyone's trust and privacy safe.

   CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOKS:
       Under Campaigns (7) you can make a step-by-step PLAYBOOK for
       any campaign. It saves a printable instructions sheet in the
       "playbooks" folder telling you exactly what to do, in order,
       to run that campaign. Reuse it for every event.

   0 = Quit.


HOW TO IMPORT A LIST (super simple):

   - Choose 1 or 2 from the menu.
   - DRAG your spreadsheet file into the black window.
   - Press Enter.
   - Type YES to confirm.
   The program ADDS the new people. It NEVER erases what's there,
   and it saves a backup first every single time.


IMPORTANT - PLEASE READ:

   * The program does NOT send any texts or emails by itself.
     It helps you get organized. You send messages yourself,
     from your own phone, after leadership approves them.

   * We only do a home visit for someone who gave us their
     address AND said yes to a visit. If there's no address,
     we don't go to a door. That keeps everyone safe and welcome.

   * If anyone ever says "stop" or "don't contact me," we honor
     that. Do not text them again.


WHERE YOUR STUFF IS:

   data\            - your spreadsheets (contacts, members, volunteers)
   backups\         - automatic safety copies
   imports_dropbox\ - untouched copies of every file you import
